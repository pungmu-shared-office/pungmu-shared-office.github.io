Non-Overcrowded Area Tax Benefits

Benefits that apply when you register a business or incorporate in Gimpo, a Growth Management Zone

No surcharge on corporate registration license tax
In overcrowded-concentration areas such as Seoul, the registration license tax on incorporation or on relocating a head office is charged at three times the standard rate. In Gimpo, only the standard rate applies.

Overcrowded area
(Seoul etc.) 1.2% Standard 0.4% × 3
Gimpo
(non-overcrowded) 0.4% Standard rate

Your initial registration license tax is about one third of the Seoul rate.
